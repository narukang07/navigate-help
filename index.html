1Your Home Page

After you log in, you will see your Home Page. This is your starting point every time you visit.

  1. 1
    Top menu. My Learning brings you back to this Home Page. Completed Learning shows chapters you finished. Need help? opens this guide.
  2. 2
    This week's chapter. Under My Current Learning, click Start Course to begin.
  3. 3
    The program card opens the program map. It lists every chapter, like a table of contents. More about this.

On a phone or small tablet

  1. 1
    The menu is hidden behind a button with three short lines (☰). Tap it to see My Learning, Completed Learning, and Need help?

2The Program Map

On your Home Page, click the program card (the one that shows the number of courses). You will see every chapter in the program and how far along you are.

  1. 1
    The chapter that is open for you now has a red Start button.
  2. 2
    "Complete previous course" and a grey Locked button mean the chapter is not open yet.
  3. 3
    A lock (🔒) opens after you finish the chapter before it. Chapters open one at a time.

3How every chapter works

About the first chapterThe first chapter is mostly a set of basic questions about you, with a short welcome video. There are no right or wrong answers. You move through it the same way as every other chapter.

After that, every chapter has the same 3 parts, in the same order. Learn it once, and you know them all. (The pictures below come from different chapters, because they all look the same.)

Important: go in orderEach part opens only after you finish the part before it.

Step 1Open this week's chapter

On your Home Page, click Start Course. You will see the chapter page.

  1. 1
    Click the big red Start button.
  2. 2
    This list shows the 3 parts of the chapter: questions before the video, the video, and questions after the video.

Coming back later?

If you stopped in the middle, the button says Continue instead of Start.

  1. 1
    Click Continue to go back to where you stopped.
  2. 2
    A green check and green box mean that part is finished.

Step 2Answer the questions before the video

Inside the chapter, the first part opens. Here is what you will see:

  1. 1
    Click Start Assessment to see the questions.
  2. 2
    The list on the left shows the parts of the chapter. A small lock (🔒) means that part is not open yet.
  3. 3
    The extra resources below the list are optional videos and links. They open after you watch the main video.
  4. 4
    The house (⌂) takes you back to your Home Page. The three lines hide or show the list on the left.

Answering a question

  1. 1
    Click the circle next to your answer.
  2. 2
    Click Save Answer and Continue. Your answer is saved only when you click this button. Do this for each question.

When you finish the questions

  1. 1
    Click Return to Course.
  1. 1
    A green message tells you the part is done. Click Next Module at the top right.
  2. 2
    The part now has a green check.
It's not a testThese questions show what you already know. It's fine if you are not sure. Just pick your best answer.

Step 3Watch the video

  1. 1
    Video buttons. Click ▶ to play. Move your mouse over the video if you don't see the buttons.
  2. 2
    Watch until the end, then click Next Module.
  3. 3
    The questions after the video stay locked until the video is finished.

What the video buttons do (from left to right)

▶ / ❚❚Play or pause the video.
↺ 10Go back 10 seconds to hear something again.
🔊Turn the sound up, down, or on.
1xChange the speed. Choose a smaller number to slow it down.
CCTurn on captions (words on the screen).
⛶Fill the whole screen. Press Esc on your keyboard to go back.

Step 4Answer the questions after the video

These work just like the questions in Step 2:

  1. Click Start Assessment.
  2. Choose your answer, then click Save Answer and Continue.
  3. At the end, click Return to Course.

Step 5Check that you're finished

  • Look at the list on the left. All 3 parts should each have a green check.
  • If one part has no check, click it and finish it.
  • When all 3 are checked, the chapter is complete. The next chapter will appear on your Home Page.

Click the house (⌂) at the top left to go back to your Home Page.

5Common questions

Click a question to see the answer.

Why is the next chapter locked?

Chapters open one at a time. Go back to the chapter you are on and make sure every part has a green check. Most often, the video was not watched to the end, or a question was not saved.

Why can't I open the questions after the video?

They open only after you finish the video. Go back to the video and let it play to the very end.

I stopped in the middle. Will I lose my place?

No. Parts you finished are saved. Log in again, open the chapter, and click Continue.

The video won't play or there is no sound.
  • Check that your speakers are on and the volume is up.
  • Check the speaker button on the video is not muted.
  • Refresh the page: press F5, or click the circular arrow (↻) at the top of your browser.
  • Try Google Chrome if you use a different browser.
The words are too small to read.

Hold the Ctrl key (or Command ⌘ on a Mac) and press + to make everything bigger. Press − to make it smaller. On this guide, you can also use the A+ button in the bottom corner.
